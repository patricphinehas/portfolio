Kilimala

Four ways onto the land

The resort sits near Sukiriyal Waterfalls, with Chulliyar Dam, Palakkapandi, Seetharkundu, and Vellarimedu within about 4 km. The Nelliyampathy hills and Kollengode forest are the backdrop, and the Nilgiri Tahr habitat is close by.

Stay

Deluxe rooms, huts, a dormitory, and a pool on the mango farm.

Rooms and an office for a quieter stay, open huts and cottages in the orchard, dormitory beds for a group, and a restaurant beside the water.
