English · Tier-I and Tier-II English

Cloze test

A cloze test is a short paragraph with several blanks. You fill them so that grammar and meaning both survive. Do not fill blank 1 and forget what blank 1 did to the story. Read the whole paragraph once with the blanks still empty, just to catch the topic.

The notice says at least one passage may be simple narrative and others may feel like a report. The method is the same.

What SSC lists

  • Cloze passage.
  • Grammar blanks and meaning blanks in the same paragraph.
  • Articles, linkers and tense consistency across the blanks.
  • The habit of reading one sentence beyond the blank before choosing.

Rules and formulas

First reading

Read the paragraph and name the topic in four words. Then each blank has a job.

Linkers

However contrasts. Therefore gives a result. Also adds a similar point. Then marks the next event.

One tense

If the story is in the past, do not drop a present-tense verb into it unless someone is being quoted.

Local grammar

The word before the blank still matters: an before a vowel sound, since before a point of time, a singular verb after neither.

Common traps

Do not choose a word that fits the blank’s sentence and contradicts the next sentence.

A linker is not a decoration. However in a sentence that does not contrast anything is wrong.

Fill all the blanks and then read the paragraph aloud. A clash usually shows on that reading.

The same word repeated in two blanks is sometimes right, and sometimes a lazy choice. Check both jobs.

Worked examples

Read the solution only after you have tried the question. Options are in exam style.

Example 1
“Ravi opened the notebook ______ began to write.” The blank is best filled by

  1. but
  2. and
  3. although
  4. unless

Solution

  1. He did two actions in a row: opened, then wrote.
  2. And joins them.
  3. But or although would need a contrast that is not there.

Answer: B. Unless would need a condition.

Example 2
“The shop was shut. ______, people kept joining the queue.” The blank is best filled by

  1. Therefore
  2. However
  3. For example
  4. In other words

Solution

  1. A shut shop should make people leave the queue.
  2. Instead the queue grew, which is the opposite of what you expect, so however fits.
  3. Therefore would claim the shut shop caused people to join, which does not follow.

Answer: B. However marks the surprise.

Example 3
“She has lived in Jaipur ______ 2019.” The blank is

  1. for
  2. since
  3. by
  4. on

Solution

  1. 2019 is a starting point.
  2. Has lived pairs with since for a point of time.

Answer: B. For would need a duration, such as six years.

Example 4
“Neither of the answers ______ correct, so she tried the question again.” The blank is

  1. were
  2. are
  3. was
  4. have

Solution

  1. Neither is singular.
  2. The story uses tried, a past verb, so was matches the time.
  3. Were would be plural.

Answer: C. The later verb tried keeps the paragraph in the past.

Example 5
(CGL pattern) Children ______ reading skills were weak needed extra classes.

  1. which
  2. whose
  3. whom
  4. who

Solution

  1. The blank shows possession: the children’s reading skills.
  2. Whose is the possessive relative pronoun.

Answer: B. Who would need a verb right after it.

Practice yourself

Attempt these without opening the answer. Then check the toggle.

Practice 1
“He was tired, ______ he finished the set.”

  1. so
  2. because
  3. yet
  4. or
Show answer

Answer: C. Yet marks the contrast between being tired and still finishing.

Practice 2
“______ the rain, the match continued.”

  1. Although
  2. Despite
  3. Because
  4. Unless
Show answer

Answer: B. Despite is followed by a noun, the rain. Although would need a clause.

Practice 3
“A bird ______ sitting on the wire when I looked out.”

  1. is
  2. was
  3. were
  4. are
Show answer

Answer: B. Looked is past, and one bird takes was.

Practice 4
“The plan is simple. ______, it will take time.”

  1. Therefore
  2. For example
  3. However
  4. Similarly
Show answer

Answer: C. Simple would suggest speed. Taking time contrasts with that, so however.
