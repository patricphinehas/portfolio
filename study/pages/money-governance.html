Money, banking and governance

The last part of Paper-III joins the economy to the institutions an audit officer actually meets: the RBI, the Budget, the Finance Commission, the CAG, and the FRBM Act. It closes with the role of information technology in governance.

Learn the deficit identities. They are the questions that look like maths and are really definitions.

What SSC lists

  • Monetary and fiscal policy, the RBI, commercial banks, regional rural banks and payments banks.
  • Budget deficits and the balance of payments.
  • The FRBM Act, 2003.
  • The Finance Commission, the CAG, and information technology in governance.

Rules and formulas

RBI and banks

The RBI issues currency, acts as banker to the government and to banks, manages foreign exchange reserves, and uses monetary policy to influence credit.
A commercial bank accepts deposits and gives loans.
A regional rural bank serves rural credit. A payments bank can take deposits and move payments, within the limits of its licence. It is not a full commercial bank. Check the RBI’s current directions if a question is about a limit, because limits are amended.

Deficits

Revenue deficit = revenue expenditure − revenue receipts.
Fiscal deficit = total expenditure − (revenue receipts + non-debt capital receipts). It is the borrowing requirement.
Primary deficit = fiscal deficit − interest payments.
The balance of payments has a current account, for trade, services and transfers, and a capital account, for investment and loans.

Laws and bodies

The FRBM Act, 2003, sets a framework for fiscal discipline. The numerical targets have been amended, so quote a target only from the current text.
The Finance Commission, Article 280, recommends how taxes are shared between the Union and the states. It is constituted every five years.
The Comptroller and Auditor General, Article 148, audits the accounts of the Union and the states.

Technology

Information technology in governance means delivering and recording public services digitally: payments, records, and audit trails.
Scheme names change. Revise the current digital programmes from a government source rather than from a fixed list.

Quick methods

Fiscal deficit = total expenditure − revenue receipts − non-debt capital receipts.
Primary deficit = fiscal deficit − interest payments.
Revenue deficit = revenue expenditure − revenue receipts.

Common traps

The fiscal deficit is not the same as the revenue deficit. The fiscal deficit includes the capital account.

The primary deficit removes interest. It answers “what is the gap before the cost of old debt?”

The Finance Commission recommends. It does not pass the Budget. Parliament does.

The CAG audits. The CAG does not prepare the government’s budget.

Worked examples

Read the solution only after you have tried the question. Options are in exam style.

Example 1
The fiscal deficit is

  1. revenue expenditure minus revenue receipts
  2. the government’s borrowing requirement
  3. exports minus imports
  4. the repo rate

Solution

  1. It is what must be borrowed after revenue receipts and non-debt capital receipts.
  2. The revenue deficit is the narrower gap.

Answer: B. Option A is the revenue deficit.

Example 2
The primary deficit equals

  1. fiscal deficit minus interest payments
  2. revenue deficit plus interest
  3. GDP minus GNP
  4. tax revenue only

Solution

  1. Interest on past debt is removed.
  2. What remains is the primary deficit.

Answer: A. A primary surplus would mean the fiscal deficit is smaller than the interest bill.

Example 3
The Finance Commission is constituted under

  1. Article 148
  2. Article 280
  3. Article 324
  4. Article 110

Solution

  1. Article 280 provides the Finance Commission.
  2. Article 148 is the CAG.

Answer: B. It is set up every five years.

Example 4
The current account of the balance of payments includes

  1. trade in goods and services and transfers
  2. only foreign direct investment
  3. only the fiscal deficit
  4. only minting of coins

Solution

  1. Goods, services and transfers are current.
  2. Investment and loans are capital-account items.

Answer: A. Foreign direct investment is a capital flow.
