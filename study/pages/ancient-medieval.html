Ancient and medieval India

Ancient and medieval questions reward a few clear anchors: the Indus cities, Ashoka, the Gupta-period names textbooks repeat, the five Delhi sultanate dynasties, and the start of Mughal rule at Panipat. Dates that scholars still debate, such as the exact year of the Buddha’s birth, are a poor use of memory. Learn the placement, not a false precision.

High-yield beats a king-list you cannot revise.

What SSC lists

  • History of India, ancient and medieval, inside General Awareness.
  • Indus valley, Vedic and classical-age anchors, Buddhism and Jainism.
  • Delhi sultanate and the early Mughals.
  • Culture of these periods is continued on the culture page.

Rules and formulas

Indus

Harappa and Mohenjo-daro are the two city names asked most often. The Great Bath is at Mohenjo-daro.
The cities show planned streets and drainage. Iron is not a feature of this civilisation in the standard account.

Later ancient

Ashoka’s turn toward the Buddha’s teaching is tied to the Kalinga war, and his edicts are inscribed sources.
Textbooks call the Gupta period a classical age and associate Kalidasa and Aryabhata with it.
Gautama Buddha’s teaching is summarised as the Four Noble Truths and the Eightfold Path. Mahavira is the name tied to Jainism in these questions.

Sultanate

The Delhi sultanate dynasties, in the usual textbook order, are the Slave (Mamluk), Khalji, Tughlaq, Sayyid and Lodi dynasties.

Mughals and the south

The first Battle of Panipat, 1526, was Babur’s defeat of Ibrahim Lodi and the start of Mughal power in north India.
The second Battle of Panipat, 1556, confirmed Akbar’s position against Hemu.
Ain-i-Akbari is associated with Abul Fazl. Krishnadevaraya is the Vijayanagara ruler these papers name most often.

Common traps

The Great Bath is Mohenjo-daro, not Harappa.

Panipat happened three times. 1526 is Babur, 1556 is Akbar’s side against Hemu, and 1761 is the Marathas and Ahmad Shah Abdali. Do not attach Babur to 1761.

The Buddha’s precise birth year is disputed. A paper that wants a century is safer to treat carefully; prefer the teachings and the later Ashokan link, which are stable.

Slave, Khalji, Tughlaq, Sayyid, Lodi is an order. Swapping Khalji and Tughlaq is a common miss.

Worked examples

Read the solution only after you have tried the question. Options are in exam style.

Example 1
The Great Bath is associated with

  1. Harappa
  2. Mohenjo-daro
  3. Lothal only as a dock
  4. Pataliputra

Solution

  1. The Great Bath is a structure at Mohenjo-daro.
  2. Lothal is known in textbooks for a dockyard, which is a different site-fact.

Answer: B. Harappa is the other great city, but it is not the Bath.

Example 2
The first Battle of Panipat was fought in

  1. 1526
  2. 1556
  3. 1761
  4. 1857

Solution

  1. 1526: Babur and Ibrahim Lodi.
  2. 1556 is the second battle. 1761 is the third.

Answer: A. 1857 is the revolt, not Panipat.

Example 3
Ain-i-Akbari is associated with

  1. Abul Fazl
  2. Ibn Battuta
  3. Amir Khusrau
  4. Todar Mal as its author

Solution

  1. Abul Fazl wrote the Akbarnama, of which Ain-i-Akbari is the part these papers name.
  2. Todar Mal is associated with revenue administration, not with authoring that book.

Answer: A. Keep the book and the revenue minister as two different facts.

Example 4
The usual textbook order of the Delhi sultanate dynasties begins

  1. Lodi, Sayyid, Tughlaq
  2. Slave, Khalji, Tughlaq
  3. Khalji, Slave, Mughal
  4. Tughlaq, Slave, Khalji

Solution

  1. The early sequence is Slave, then Khalji, then Tughlaq.
  2. Sayyid and Lodi come after Tughlaq.

Answer: B. The Mughals are not a Delhi sultanate dynasty in that list.

Example 5
(CGL pattern) Prabhavatigupta, daughter of Chandragupta II, was married into which dynasty?

  1. Vakataka
  2. Pallava
  3. Chalukya
  4. Pushyabhuti

Solution

  1. She married the Vakataka king Rudrasena II.
  2. The alliance strengthened Gupta influence in the Deccan.

Answer: A. Pushyabhuti is Harsha’s line, much later.

Example 6
(CGL pattern) Which Delhi Sultanate department, dealing with the army, did Balban reorganise?

  1. Diwan-i-Insha
  2. Diwan-i-Arz
  3. Diwan-i-Risalat
  4. Diwan-i-Mustakhraj

Solution

  1. Diwan-i-Arz was the military department.
  2. Diwan-i-Mustakhraj (arrears) is linked to Alauddin Khalji.

Answer: B. Arz = army; Insha = correspondence.
