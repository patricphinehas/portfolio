English · Tier-I and Tier-II English

Synonyms and antonyms

A synonym is a word close in meaning. An antonym is a word opposite in meaning. The question will say which one it wants. Read that word before you look at the options, or you will pick a synonym when an antonym was asked.

Context still matters. A word can have two senses, and only one sense fits the sentence.

What SSC lists

  • Synonyms and antonyms, and their correct usage.
  • Homonyms are practised on the spelling page, because they are words that look or sound alike.
  • Vocabulary in short sentences.
  • The same words reappear in cloze tests and reading questions.

Rules and formulas

How to choose

Replace the given word with the option inside a simple sentence. If the sentence still means the same thing, you have a synonym.

Antonym check

The antonym should reverse the sentence, not merely change the topic.

Pairs worth knowing

Abundant / plentiful. Brief / short. Cautious / careful. Ancient / old.
Scarce is an antonym of abundant. Artificial is an antonym of natural. Permanent is an antonym of temporary.

Usage

A synonym is not always interchangeable in tone. Kid and child are close, but a formal sentence wants child.

Common traps

Read whether the question says “similar” or “opposite”.

A near miss is still wrong: big is not the synonym of enormous if vast is an option and the sentence is about size in a formal tone. Pick the closest.

Do not choose an antonym that is only negative in feeling if the meanings are not opposites.

Learn words in a short phrase, not as a bare list.

Worked examples

Read the solution only after you have tried the question. Options are in exam style.

Example 1
Choose the synonym of abundant.

  1. scarce
  2. plentiful
  3. empty
  4. narrow

Solution

  1. Abundant means present in a large quantity.
  2. Plentiful has the same sense.

Answer: B. Scarce is the opposite, and it is there to catch a misread question.

Example 2
Choose the antonym of abundant.

  1. plentiful
  2. rich
  3. scarce
  4. enough

Solution

  1. The question now asks for the opposite.
  2. Scarce means not enough.

Answer: C. Plentiful would have been the synonym.

Example 3
Choose the synonym of brief.

  1. long
  2. short
  3. late
  4. loud

Solution

  1. A brief speech is a short speech.
  2. Short is the synonym.

Answer: B. Long is the antonym.

Example 4
Choose the synonym of cautious.

  1. careless
  2. hurried
  3. careful
  4. certain

Solution

  1. A cautious person takes care to avoid risk.
  2. Careful is the close synonym.

Answer: C. Careless reverses the meaning.

Example 5
Choose the antonym of temporary.

  1. brief
  2. permanent
  3. sudden
  4. partial

Solution

  1. Temporary means lasting for a limited time.
  2. Permanent reverses that.

Answer: B. Brief is closer to a synonym than to an antonym.

Example 6
(CGL pattern) Select the synonym of the bracketed word to fill the blank: The report was full of ______ (misleading) claims.

  1. sincere
  2. candid
  3. mendacious
  4. true

Solution

  1. Mendacious means untruthful or misleading.
  2. Sincere, candid and true are near opposites.

Answer: C. Learn a few hard synonyms: mendacious, taintless, astute.

Example 7
(CGL pattern) Select the antonym of the bracketed word to fill the blank: The ______ to the hall was decorated with lights. [exit]

  1. entrance
  2. roof
  3. road
  4. ceremony

Solution

  1. The opposite of exit is entrance.
  2. It also fits the sentence.

Answer: A. The answer must be the antonym and fit the blank.
