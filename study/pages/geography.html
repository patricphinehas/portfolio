Geography

Geography questions mix the map of India with a little physical geography: rivers, soil, monsoon and neighbours. Learn the map as routes, not as a list of capitals you already know from school.

A neighbouring country is a standard item. So is the Tropic of Cancer, and the fact that the Narmada and the Tapi flow west.

What SSC lists

  • Geography of India and its neighbouring countries.
  • Physical features, rivers, climate and soils.
  • A small amount of world geography that sits next to India: neighbours and basic locations.
  • Economic geography of crops is touched again on the economy page.

Rules and formulas

Lines

The Tropic of Cancer passes through eight states: Gujarat, Rajasthan, Madhya Pradesh, Chhattisgarh, Jharkhand, West Bengal, Tripura and Mizoram.
Indian Standard Time is based on 82°30′ E, about 5 hours 30 minutes ahead of GMT. Mirzapur in Uttar Pradesh is the town named with that meridian.

Rivers and relief

The Himalaya, the northern plains, the peninsular plateau, the coasts and the islands are the five large physical divisions used in school books.
The Narmada and the Tapi flow west into the Arabian Sea. The Godavari, Krishna and Kaveri flow east. The Godavari is often called the Dakshin Ganga.
Kangchenjunga is the highest peak in India in the standard answer.

Climate and soil

The south-west monsoon is the rainy season from about June to September.
Alluvial soil covers the northern plains. Black soil, also called regur, is associated with cotton and the Deccan lava region.

Neighbours

School and exam lists name seven land neighbours: Pakistan, Afghanistan, China, Nepal, Bhutan, Bangladesh and Myanmar. Sri Lanka and the Maldives are maritime neighbours. Borders in the north-west are politically disputed; the list above is the textbook answer these papers use.

Common traps

The Tropic of Cancer does not pass through Odisha or Uttar Pradesh.

West-flowing peninsular rivers in the basic list are the Narmada and the Tapi, not the Godavari.

Kangchenjunga, not Mount Everest, is the highest peak in India. Everest is in Nepal.

IST is 5 hours 30 minutes ahead, not 5 hours.

Worked examples

Read the solution only after you have tried the question. Options are in exam style.

Example 1
The Tropic of Cancer does not pass through

  1. Gujarat
  2. Rajasthan
  3. Odisha
  4. Mizoram

Solution

  1. The eight states are Gujarat, Rajasthan, Madhya Pradesh, Chhattisgarh, Jharkhand, West Bengal, Tripura and Mizoram.
  2. Odisha is not in that list.

Answer: C. West Bengal is in the list. Odisha is the state people often add by mistake.

Example 2
Which river flows west into the Arabian Sea?

  1. Godavari
  2. Krishna
  3. Kaveri
  4. Narmada

Solution

  1. The Godavari, Krishna and Kaveri flow east into the Bay of Bengal.
  2. The Narmada flows west.

Answer: D. The Tapi is the other famous west-flowing peninsular river.

Example 3
Indian Standard Time is ahead of GMT by

  1. 4 hours 30 minutes
  2. 5 hours
  3. 5 hours 30 minutes
  4. 6 hours

Solution

  1. The standard meridian is 82°30′ E.
  2. 15 degrees of longitude correspond to one hour, so 82.5 / 15 = 5.5 hours.

Answer: C. The half hour is the part that gets dropped in a hurry.

Example 4
Black regur soil is especially associated with

  1. tea in Assam
  2. cotton
  3. apples in Himachal
  4. jute in the delta only

Solution

  1. Regur is the black soil of the Deccan lava tract.
  2. Cotton is the crop paired with it in these questions.

Answer: B. Tea, apple and jute have other regions.

Example 5
(CGL pattern) The world’s largest drainage basin is the

  1. Congo basin
  2. Nile basin
  3. Amazon basin
  4. Amur basin

Solution

  1. The Amazon basin covers about 7 million km².
  2. The Nile is the longest river by the usual measure, not the largest basin.

Answer: C. Largest basin and longest river are different questions.

  2. maritime neighbour

Answer: B. The sea separates them. The textbook land-neighbour list does not include Sri Lanka.
