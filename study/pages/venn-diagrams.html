Reasoning · Tier-I and Paper-I

Venn diagrams

A Venn question asks which picture of circles matches a set of real groups. If every member of one group belongs to another, the first circle sits inside the second. If the groups can overlap, the circles cross. If they cannot share a member, the circles stay apart.

This is not a syllogism. You use ordinary knowledge of the groups, and you choose a diagram.

What SSC lists

  • Venn diagrams for three everyday groups.
  • Subset, overlap, and disjoint groups.
  • Reading a labelled diagram to answer “which are both”.
  • The same drawing skill as syllogism, with real categories.

Rules and formulas

Inside

If every A is a B, draw A inside B. Every dog is an animal, so Dogs sits inside Animals.

Overlap

If some but not all can be both, the circles cross. Some teachers are men, and some are not.

Apart

If nobody can be both, the circles do not touch. A person is not both a son and a daughter.

Three groups

Decide each pair first. Then draw the third circle.

Common traps

Do not put Dogs inside Cats. They are both inside Animals, and they do not overlap.

Brothers and sisters are both inside siblings, and they do not overlap each other.

A diagram question is about possible membership, not about a conclusion that “follows”.

Read the labels. The same three circles mean different things if the labels move.

Worked examples

Read the solution only after you have tried the question. Options are in exam style.

Example 1
Which description fits Dogs, Cats and Animals?

  1. Three separate circles
  2. Dogs and Cats are separate circles inside Animals
  3. Dogs inside Cats inside Animals
  4. Cats inside Dogs, and Animals separate

Solution

  1. Every dog is an animal, and every cat is an animal.
  2. A dog is not a cat.
  3. Two separate circles inside a larger one.

Answer: B. The large circle is Animals.

Example 2
Which description fits Mothers, Women and Sisters?

  1. Three separate circles
  2. Mothers and Sisters overlap, and both sit inside Women
  3. Sisters inside Mothers inside Women, with no overlap the other way
  4. Women inside Mothers

Solution

  1. Every mother in this ordinary grouping is a woman, and every sister is a woman.
  2. A woman can be both a mother and a sister, so those two circles cross inside Women.

Answer: B. Not every sister is a mother, so Sisters is not inside Mothers.

Example 3
Sun, Moon and Earth, as three objects, are best drawn as

  1. three separate circles
  2. Moon inside Earth inside Sun
  3. Earth inside Sun, Moon separate
  4. one single circle

Solution

  1. None of these objects is a kind of one of the others.
  2. The circles stay apart.

Answer: A. An orbit is not a Venn subset.

Example 4
Which description fits Teachers, Men and Women?

  1. Men and Women overlap each other, and Teachers sits apart
  2. Men and Women are separate, and Teachers crosses both
  3. All men are inside Teachers
  4. Teachers is inside Men only

Solution

  1. Men and women, as these categories, do not overlap.
  2. A teacher may be a man or a woman, so the teacher circle crosses both.

Answer: B. Not every man is a teacher, so Men is not inside Teachers.

Example 5
(CGL pattern) In a Venn diagram of Smart, Polite and Honest people: Smart only = 12, Polite only = 20, Smart and Polite but not Honest = 7, all three = 5. How many smart people are polite?

  1. 7
  2. 12
  3. 19
  4. 5

Solution

  1. Smart and polite includes the part outside Honest and the centre.
  2. 7 + 5 = 12.

Answer: B. Do not forget the centre region shared by all three.

Practice 1
Odd numbers and even numbers, inside whole numbers, are drawn as

  1. two overlapping circles
  2. two separate circles inside a larger circle
  3. one circle
  4. odd numbers inside even numbers
Show answer

Answer: B. No whole number is both odd and even.

Practice 2
Squares and rectangles: every square is a rectangle, so the drawing is

  1. squares inside rectangles
  2. rectangles inside squares
  3. two separate circles
  4. one circle with no subset
Show answer

Answer: A. A square is a special rectangle. The converse is not true.

Practice 3
Goldfish, fish and birds are drawn as

  1. goldfish inside fish, birds separate
  2. birds inside fish
  3. all three separate
  4. fish inside goldfish
Show answer

Answer: A. A goldfish is a fish. A bird is not.

Practice 4
Authors and poets: some poets are authors and some authors are not poets. The circles

  1. coincide
  2. overlap
  3. stay fully apart
  4. cannot be drawn
Show answer

Answer: B. The groups share some members and each has members the other lacks.

Practice 5
Doctors, Women and Mothers are best drawn as

  1. Mothers inside Women, and Doctors crossing both
  2. three separate circles
  3. Women inside Doctors
  4. Doctors inside Mothers
Show answer

Answer: A. Every mother is a woman. A doctor may or may not be a woman or a mother.

Practice 6
India, Delhi and Asia are best drawn as

  1. three separate circles
  2. Delhi inside India inside Asia
  3. Asia inside India
  4. India and Delhi overlapping partly
Show answer

Answer: B. Each is wholly inside the next.

Practice 7
Tea, Coffee and Beverages are best drawn as

  1. Tea inside Coffee
  2. three separate circles
  3. Tea and Coffee as separate circles inside Beverages
  4. one circle
Show answer

Answer: C. Both are beverages, and neither is the other.
