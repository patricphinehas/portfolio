Statistics (Paper-II) · Tier-II Paper-II, JSO and Statistical Investigator only

Central tendency and dispersion

Central tendency is the middle of the data: mean, median, mode, and the partition values. Dispersion is how far the data spread: range, quartile deviation, mean deviation and standard deviation.

For a list, the school formulas are enough. For a grouped table, use the class formulas. Say which one you are using.

What SSC lists

  • Mean, median and mode.
  • Quartiles, deciles and percentiles.
  • Range, quartile deviation, mean deviation and standard deviation.
  • A measure of relative dispersion, such as the coefficient of variation.

Rules and formulas

Ungrouped

Mean = sum / n.
Median: sort the list. If n is odd, the middle value. If n is even, the average of the two middle values.
For a simple sorted list, a common position formula is the ((n+1)/4)th value for Q1 and the (3(n+1)/4)th for Q3.

Grouped

Mean = Σ(f × midpoint) / Σf.
Median = L + ((N/2 − cf) / f) × h, where L is the lower boundary of the median class, cf is the cumulative frequency before it, f is its frequency and h is its width.
Mode = L + ((f1 − f0) / (2f1 − f0 − f2)) × h, with f1 the modal frequency and f0, f2 the neighbours.

Spread

Range = maximum − minimum.
Quartile deviation = (Q3 − Q1) / 2.
Mean deviation about the mean = Σ|x − mean| / n.
Standard deviation, population form used in many of these papers: √[Σ(x − mean)² / n]. Variance is the square of that.

Relative

Coefficient of variation = (standard deviation / mean) × 100, when the mean is not zero.
Use it to compare spread of two series that have different units or different means. A smaller CV is more consistent.

Quick methods

Assumed mean: mean = A + Σfd/Σf, with d = x − A.
Step deviation: mean = A + (Σfu/Σf) × h, with u = (x − A)/h.
Add a constant → mean shifts, SD unchanged. Multiply by k → SD × |k|, variance × k².
Two values a and b: SD = |a − b|/2. First n natural numbers: variance = (n² − 1)/12.

Common traps

The grouped median formula uses N/2, not (N+1)/2.

Quartile deviation uses half the gap between Q3 and Q1, not the whole gap.

Standard deviation is the square root. Variance is not.

The empirical relation mode ≈ 3 median − 2 mean is an approximation for a moderately skewed set. It is not an identity. Paper-I also uses it.

Worked examples

Read the solution only after you have tried the question. Options are in exam style.

Example 1
The mean of 4, 8, 10 and 18 is

  1. 8
  2. 10
  3. 12
  4. 40

Solution

  1. Sum = 40.
  2. Mean = 40/4 = 10.

Answer: B. 40 is the sum, which is the trap.

Example 2
The median of 7, 2, 9, 4, 5 is

  1. 2
  2. 5
  3. 7
  4. 9

Solution

  1. Sort: 2, 4, 5, 7, 9.
  2. The middle of five values is 5.

Answer: B. Do not take the middle of the unsorted list.

Example 3
For the sorted list 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, using positions (n+1)/4 and 3(n+1)/4, the quartile deviation is

  1. 1
  2. 2
  3. 4
  4. 6

Solution

  1. n = 7, so (n+1)/4 = 2 and 3(n+1)/4 = 6.
  2. Q1 is the 2nd value, 2. Q3 is the 6th value, 6.
  3. Quartile deviation = (6 − 2) / 2 = 2.

Answer: B. The range would be 6. The quartile deviation is half of Q3 − Q1.

Example 4
The standard deviation of 1, 1, 5, 5, dividing by n, is

  1. 0
  2. 2
  3. 4
  4. 8

Solution

  1. Mean = 3.
  2. Squared deviations sum to 16. Variance = 16/4 = 4.
  3. Standard deviation = 2.

Answer: B. 4 is the variance. The question asks for the root.

Answer: B. (4/40) × 100 = 10%.

Practice 4
A lower coefficient of variation means

  1. a larger mean only
  2. greater consistency
  3. a larger range always
  4. a bimodal list
Show answer

Answer: B. Relative spread is smaller.

Practice 5
The standard deviation of 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 is

  1. 2
  2. √2
  3. 1
  4. √10
Show answer

Answer: B. Variance = (25 − 1)/12 = 2, so SD = √2.

Practice 6
Every value is increased by 5. The SD was 4. The new SD is

  1. 4
  2. 9
  3. 20
  4. 1
Show answer

Answer: A. A shift of origin does not change spread.

Practice 7
Every value is multiplied by 3. The variance was 4. The new variance is

  1. 12
  2. 36
  3. 4
  4. 16
Show answer

Answer: B. Variance × 3² = 36.

Practice 8
Assumed mean 50, Σf = 20, Σfd = 40. The mean is

  1. 48
  2. 50
  3. 52
  4. 90
Show answer

Answer: C. 50 + 40/20 = 52.
