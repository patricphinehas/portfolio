Reasoning · Tier-I and Paper-I

Direction sense

Direction questions are a walk on a square grid, or a person turning left and right. North and south are opposite. East and west are opposite. A left turn from north faces west. A right turn from north faces east.

The notice places this under space orientation. Draw a small cross and mark each leg of the walk. The final question is usually the distance from the start, or the direction the person faces.

What SSC lists

  • Space orientation and direction of a walk.
  • Left and right turns from a facing direction.
  • Shortest distance back to the start, often a Pythagoras triple.
  • Shadow questions in the morning or evening, used as a facing clue.

Rules and formulas

Turns

Face north. Left → west. Right → east.
Face east. Left → north. Right → south.
Two left turns reverse the direction you face.

Distance

If the legs are perpendicular, distance from the start is √(east-west result² + north-south result²).
A 3-4-5 triangle is the usual clean result.

Net movement

North and south cancel. East and west cancel. What is left is the net shift.

Shadows

In the morning the sun is in the east, so shadows fall toward the west.
In the evening the sun is in the west, so shadows fall toward the east.

Common traps

Left and right depend on the way the person is facing, not on the top of your page.

A walk north and then the same distance south ends on the east-west line through the start.

The shortest distance is not the sum of the legs, unless the walk never turned.

“Turns left, then left, then right” is three turns. Count them on the cross.

Worked examples

Read the solution only after you have tried the question. Options are in exam style.

Example 1
A person walks 5 km north, 3 km east and 5 km south. Where is the person, relative to the start?

  1. 3 km west
  2. 3 km east
  3. 5 km east
  4. 8 km north-east

Solution

  1. The 5 km north and 5 km south cancel.
  2. The only leftover leg is 3 km east.

Answer: B. The person is due east of the start, not north-east.

Example 2
A man faces east. He turns left, then left again, then right. He now faces

  1. east
  2. west
  3. north
  4. south

Solution

  1. From east, the first left faces him north.
  2. The second left faces him west.
  3. A right turn from west faces him north.

Answer: C. Three turns: east → north → west → north.

Example 3
A person walks 4 km south and then 3 km east. The shortest distance from the start is

  1. 5 km
  2. 7 km
  3. 1 km
  4. 12 km

Solution

  1. The legs are perpendicular.
  2. Distance = √(4² + 3²) = √(16+9) = √25 = 5 km.

Answer: A. 7 km is the distance walked, not the straight-line distance.

Example 4
Early in the morning a person walks so that their shadow falls to their left. The person is walking toward

  1. east
  2. west
  3. north
  4. south

Solution

  1. Morning shadows fall toward the west.
  2. The shadow is on the person’s left, so west is to the left.
  3. The person is facing north.

Answer: C. If the person faced south, the morning shadow would fall to the right.
