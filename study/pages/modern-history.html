General Awareness · Tier-I and Paper-I General Awareness

Modern Indian history

Modern history, from the eighteenth century to 1947, is the highest-yield history block. The papers like a year tied to a movement: Plassey, 1857, the Congress, Non-Cooperation, Dandi, Quit India.

Learn each year with one name and one reason, not as a bare number.

What SSC lists

  • Modern Indian history and the national movement.
  • Company power, the revolt of 1857, and social reform.
  • Congress, mass movements, and independence.
  • The cultural side of the same years is on the culture page.

Rules and formulas

Company and revolt

The Battle of Plassey was in 1757. The Battle of Buxar was in 1764.
The revolt of 1857 is the break that ended Company rule in the textbook story and led to the Crown taking over in 1858.

Reform

Raja Ram Mohan Roy is associated with the Brahmo Samaj, founded in 1828.
Dayananda Saraswati is associated with the Arya Samaj, 1875.

Congress to 1920

The Indian National Congress was founded in 1885. A.O. Hume is the official associated with that founding in exam questions.
The Partition of Bengal was in 1905. The Muslim League was founded in 1906 at Dhaka. The Congress split at Surat in 1907.
Jallianwala Bagh was on 13 April 1919. The Non-Cooperation Movement was launched in 1920 and called off after Chauri Chaura in 1922.

Toward independence

The Lahore session of 1929 demanded Purna Swaraj. The Dandi March and Civil Disobedience belong to 1930.
The Quit India Movement was launched in August 1942.
Independence was on 15 August 1947. The Constitution came into force on 26 January 1950.

Common traps

Plassey is 1757 and Buxar is 1764. They are not the same battle.

Non-Cooperation was called off. It did not run on into the 1930s. Civil Disobedience is the 1930 movement.

26 January was the Purna Swaraj pledge day from 1930, and later the date the Constitution came into force. 15 August is Independence Day.

Quit India is 1942, not 1947.

Worked examples

Read the solution only after you have tried the question. Options are in exam style.

Example 1
The Battle of Plassey was fought in

  1. 1757
  2. 1764
  3. 1857
  4. 1526

Solution

  1. Plassey is 1757.
  2. Buxar is 1764.

Answer: A. 1526 is Panipat and 1857 is the revolt.

Example 2
The Indian National Congress was founded in

  1. 1857
  2. 1885
  3. 1905
  4. 1920

Solution

  1. The founding year used in these papers is 1885.
  2. 1905 is the Partition of Bengal and 1920 is Non-Cooperation.

Answer: B. A.O. Hume is the name usually paired with 1885.

Example 3
The Non-Cooperation Movement was called off after

  1. Jallianwala Bagh
  2. Chauri Chaura
  3. the Dandi March
  4. the Surat split

Solution

  1. Chauri Chaura was in 1922.
  2. Gandhi then called off Non-Cooperation.
  3. Dandi is 1930, a later movement.

Answer: B. Jallianwala Bagh, 1919, came before the movement, and it is one reason the movement began, not the reason it was withdrawn.

Example 4
The Quit India Movement was launched in

  1. 1930
  2. 1942
  3. 1946
  4. 1947

Solution

  1. Quit India is August 1942.
  2. 1930 is Civil Disobedience. 1947 is independence.

Answer: B. Do not collapse the last decade of the movement into one year.
