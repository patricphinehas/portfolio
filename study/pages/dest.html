Computer and DEST · Tier-II Computer Knowledge (qualifying)

Data entry speed test

DEST is Section-IV of Paper-I, a second session on the same day. You type a passage. The notice says the task is about 2000 key depressions in 15 minutes. It is qualifying. It is not a multiple-choice paper, and it is not added to the merit marks.

Some posts want a higher standard, including Tax Assistant and the posts for which computer proficiency is prescribed. The exact higher bar is set by the Commission, so this page does not invent a cutoff. Candidates eligible for a scribe may get extra time as the notice states, and some PwBD candidates have a typed medical exemption that the notice limits by post. Read para 13.9.10 before you rely on an exemption.

What SSC lists

  • Data Entry Speed Test: one passage, about 2000 key depressions, 15 minutes.
  • Qualifying in nature, with a higher standard for posts where DEST or computer proficiency is prescribed.
  • Accuracy first, then speed.
  • This is a skill, so the practice is typing, not a formula.

Rules and formulas

The task

About 2000 key depressions in 15 minutes.
2000 in 15 minutes is 8000 key depressions per hour if you keep that pace.
A key depression is a key press, not a word. Spaces and punctuation count.

How to practise

Use a simple editor. Type a clean passage for 15 minutes.
Do not look at the keyboard if you can avoid it. Fix the habit of going back to correct every word if that destroys your speed; the evaluation method is published by the Commission and regional offices, so follow their instruction on the day.

Accuracy

A wrong character costs more than a missing flourish. Read a line, then type it, then glance back.
Keep a consistent rhythm rather than a sprint and a stall.

On the day

Session-II is after a break and a fresh registration. Be early.
The passage will be in the form the Commission sets. Practise both punctuation and numbers, because a digit is a key depression too.

Common traps

DEST is not optional for ordinary posts. It is mandatory and qualifying, with the exemption rules in the notice.

2000 key depressions are not 2000 words.

A higher standard for some posts is real. This page does not know the number the Commission will fix for your cycle.

Computer Knowledge and DEST are different sections. Clearing one does not clear the other.

Worked examples

Read the solution only after you have tried the question. Options are in exam style.

Example 1
The DEST passage in the 2026 notice is about

  1. 200 key depressions
  2. 2000 key depressions
  3. 2000 words
  4. 20 multiple-choice questions

Solution

  1. The notice says about 2000 key depressions.
  2. The time is 15 minutes.
  3. The 20 questions belong to the Computer Knowledge section, not to DEST.

Answer: B. A key depression is a key press.

Example 2
For most posts, DEST is

  1. added in full to the merit total
  2. qualifying
  3. a replacement for the English paper
  4. held only for JSO

Solution

  1. Section-IV is qualifying.
  2. Merit for most posts uses Section-I and Section-II of Paper-I, after you qualify Computer Knowledge and DEST.

Answer: B. JSO has an extra statistics paper. DEST is still part of Paper-I for the posts that must take it.

Example 3
2000 key depressions in 15 minutes, kept up for an hour, would be

  1. 2000 per hour
  2. 4000 per hour
  3. 8000 per hour
  4. 15000 per hour

Solution

  1. 15 minutes is a quarter of an hour.
  2. 2000 × 4 = 8000.

Answer: C. This is pace, not a cutoff.

Example 4
Which practice matches the test?

  1. Only reading about keyboards
  2. Typing a passage against a 15-minute clock
  3. Memorising 20 computer abbreviations instead
  4. Skipping punctuation

Solution

  1. The test is a typed passage.
  2. A timed passage is the rehearsal.

Answer: B. Abbreviations help the multiple-choice section. They do not train your hands.

Practice yourself

Attempt these without opening the answer. Then check the toggle.

Practice 1
DEST is conducted in

  1. the middle of the maths section
  2. Session-II, after Session-I
  3. Paper-II
  4. the interview
Show answer

Answer: B. Session-I is the objective sections. Session-II is the data-entry task.

Practice 2
A key depression counts

  1. only letters
  2. key presses, including spaces
  3. only capital letters
  4. only correct words of five letters
Show answer

Answer: B. Spaces and other keys you press are depressions. The evaluation detail on the day follows the Commission’s instruction.

Practice 3
Posts such as Tax Assistant are associated in the notice with

  1. no DEST at all
  2. a higher DEST standard
  3. a statistics paper instead of typing
  4. exemption for every candidate
Show answer

Answer: B. The notice names a higher standard for posts where DEST is prescribed, including Tax Assistant. The numeric bar is the Commission’s.

Practice 4
The best order of priorities while learning DEST is

  1. speed so high that most words are wrong
  2. accuracy, then a steady speed
  3. looking at the keyboard for every letter
  4. typing only your name
Show answer

Answer: B. An accurate steady pace is what you can raise. A fast error-filled habit is harder to repair.
