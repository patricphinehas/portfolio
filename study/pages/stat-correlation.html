Correlation and regression

Correlation measures how two variables move together. It does not, by itself, say that one causes the other. Regression is the line you use to predict one variable from the other.

The syllabus includes rank correlation, association of attributes, and multiple and partial correlation for three variables. Know what those names mean, and be fluent with the two-variable formulas.

What SSC lists

  • Scatter diagram and the simple correlation coefficient.
  • The two regression lines.
  • Spearman’s rank correlation.
  • Association of attributes, and the idea of multiple and partial correlation for three variables.

Rules and formulas

Correlation

r lies between −1 and 1.
r = 1 is a perfect positive line. r = −1 is a perfect negative line. r = 0 means no linear correlation.
One computing form is (nΣxy − ΣxΣy) / √[(nΣx² − (Σx)²)(nΣy² − (Σy)²)].

Regression

The line of y on x predicts y from x: (y − mean of y) = b_yx (x − mean of x).
b_yx = r × (s_y / s_x). b_xy = r × (s_x / s_y).
b_yx × b_xy = r².
The two lines meet at the point of the two means.

Ranks

Spearman’s coefficient, with no ties: 1 − 6Σd² / [n(n² − 1)], where d is the difference of ranks.
Tied ranks need a corrected formula. Do not ignore a tie if the question has one.

Three variables and attributes

Multiple correlation is the link between one variable and the best linear combination of the others.
Partial correlation is the link between two variables after the third is held fixed.
Attributes are categories. Association asks whether two categories occur together more than independence would suggest. Yule’s coefficient is one measure you will meet in a full textbook.

Quick methods

r = cov(x, y)/(σx σy), with cov = Σ(x − x̄)(y − ȳ)/n.
r = ±√(b_yx × b_xy), taking the common sign of the two coefficients.
b_yx and b_xy always share a sign.
Change of origin, or of scale by positive factors, leaves r unchanged.
Estimate: ŷ = ȳ + b_yx(x − x̄).

Common traps

A high correlation is not a cause.

There are two regression lines. The line of y on x is not the algebraic reverse of the line of x on y unless the fit is perfect.

r² is the product of the two regression coefficients, and it cannot be negative. r can.

Spearman’s basic formula assumes no ties.

Worked examples

Read the solution only after you have tried the question. Options are in exam style.

Example 1
The correlation coefficient must lie between

  1. 0 and 1
  2. −1 and 1
  3. −2 and 2
  4. 0 and 100

Solution

  1. Negative values are allowed.
  2. The bounds are −1 and 1.

Answer: B. A value of 1.2 is a calculation error.

Example 2
If the two regression coefficients are 0.8 and 0.45, then r² is

  1. 0.36
  2. 1.25
  3. 0.8
  4. 0.45

Solution

  1. The product of the regression coefficients is r².
  2. 0.8 × 0.45 = 0.36.

Answer: A. r would be 0.6 or −0.6. The squares do not keep the sign. Here both coefficients are positive, so r is positive.

Example 3
For ranks with Σd² = 0, Spearman’s coefficient is

  1. 0
  2. 1
  3. −1
  4. 0.5

Solution

  1. The formula is 1 minus a multiple of Σd².
  2. If every rank difference is 0, the coefficient is 1.

Answer: B. The two rankings agree perfectly.

Example 4
For n = 3, rank differences that give Σd² = 2, Spearman’s coefficient is

  1. 0
  2. 0.5
  3. 1
  4. −0.5

Solution

  1. Denominator n(n² − 1) = 3 × 8 = 24.
  2. 6 × 2 / 24 = 12/24 = 0.5.
  3. Coefficient = 1 − 0.5 = 0.5.

Answer: B. The subtraction from 1 is the whole method.

If r = 0, the product of the two regression coefficients is

  1. 1
  2. 0
  3. −1
  4. undefined always
Show answer

Answer: B. The product of the two coefficients is r², so it is 0.

Practice 4
Partial correlation, for three variables, holds

  1. nothing fixed
  2. the third variable fixed
  3. the sample size at 2
  4. the mean at zero only
Show answer

Answer: B. The third variable is held constant while the other two are related.

Practice 5
cov(x, y) = 12, σx = 3, σy = 5. r is

  1. 0.4
  2. 0.8
  3. 1.25
  4. 0.6
Show answer

Answer: B. 12/(3 × 5) = 0.8.

Practice 6
b_yx = −0.9 and b_xy = −0.4. r is

  1. 0.36
  2. 0.6
  3. −0.6
  4. −0.36
Show answer

Answer: C. √0.36 = 0.6, with the common negative sign.

Practice 7
x̄ = 10, ȳ = 20, b_yx = 1.5. The estimate of y at x = 14 is

  1. 21
  2. 26
  3. 30
  4. 35
Show answer

Answer: B. 20 + 1.5 × 4 = 26.
