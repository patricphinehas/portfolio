How to study with this

Do one topic page, close it, and solve the practice set on paper before you open the answers. In Quant and Reasoning, write the formula once from memory after the examples. In English, say the corrected sentence aloud. In General Awareness, treat the page as a spine and add current affairs from a fresh source every week. Computer and the typing test are qualifying: do not ignore them, and do not spend your best revision week only on them if Paper-I maths and English are still weak.

Exam shape

Taken from the Staff Selection Commission notice for Combined Graduate Level Examination, 2026 (F. No. HQ-C11018/1/2026-C-1). Tier-I is used to shortlist. For most posts, final merit uses Section-I and Section-II of Paper-I, after you qualify Computer Knowledge and DEST. There is no published cutoff in this guide.

Sectional time. Tier-I is one hour with a 15-minute timer for each of the four subjects. In Paper-I, a section closes when its time ends: maths and reasoning get 30 minutes each, English gets 40 minutes, General Awareness gets 20 minutes, then Computer Knowledge gets 15 minutes. DEST is a second session of 15 minutes on the same day. Candidates eligible for a scribe get extra time as stated in the notice. Questions are in English and Hindi except the English language sections. Maths in Tier-I is of 10th standard level; Tier-II maths is matriculation level; Tier-II English is 10+2 level; Paper-II and Paper-III are graduation level.

Who writes the extra papers. Paper-II (Statistics) is only for Junior Statistical Officer and Statistical Investigator Grade-II. Paper-III (Finance and Economics) is only for Assistant Audit Officer and Assistant Accounts Officer. If you have not chosen those posts, study Paper-I thoroughly and use the Paper-II and Paper-III pages only as optional depth.

A sensible order

  1. Percentages, ratio, average, profit and loss, interest, time-work, and time-speed. These unlock most arithmetic.
  2. Algebra identities, then geometry, mensuration, and trigonometry.
  3. Number series, coding, analogy, syllogism, and direction sense. Keep a daily set of 20.
  4. Error spotting, voice, narration, and one reading passage a day.
  5. Polity and modern history first in General Awareness, then geography and biology. Add a weekly current-affairs pass from PIB or a newspaper. Do not memorise a stale list from this site.
  6. Two weeks before Tier-II, do Computer Knowledge and a daily typing drill of about 15 minutes.
