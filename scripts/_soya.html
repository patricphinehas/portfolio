Soya 65 recipe

Written By Angela Steffi on Tuesday, June 9, 2020 | 10:38:00 PM

Posted by Angela Steffi on 10:38:00 PM
Soya chunks fry is a simple and quick recipe which goes excellent with variety rice, Meal maker is an excellent alternative for both veg and non veg lovers, it tastes really good and all we need is simple ingredients . Lets get started. 

Please click here to watch Soya 65 recipe in Tamil - Madras Samayal.


Serves              : 2 - 3

Prep Time       : 20 min 

Cook Time      : 20 minutes

Quantity Ingredients for Meal maker 65 recipe
1 cup Meal maker (60 g)
1/4 tsp Turmeric
1/2 tsp Cumin powder
1 tsp Garam masala
1 tbsp Kashmiri chili powder or 2 tsp regular
1 tbsp Ginger garlic paste
2 tbsp Rice flour
2 tbsp Besan flour
1/2  Lemon
required Salt
1 tbsp Water if required
10 Green chilies
1 spring Curry leaves

Cooking Method

Step 1) Boil water in a pan and add some salt . Add soya chunks, turn off the heat and let it sit for 10 -15 min. Rinse with cold water and squeeze any extra water. 

Step 2)  Into a mixing bowl add the softened soya , chilli powder, turmeric powder, cumin powder, ginger garlic paste , garam masala, rice flour, besan flour, required salt , juice of half lemon and mix well. If required sprinkle some water to coat all the masalas evenly. let it sit for 10 min. 

Step 3) Heat oil in a pan and fry until crispy. Turn off the heat and fry some green chilies and curry leaves. Add it to the fried soya. 

Enjoy the delicious crispy soya 65 recipe . Happy Cooking !!!
