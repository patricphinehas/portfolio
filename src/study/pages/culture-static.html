Culture and static GK

Static GK is the layer that does not change every month: classical dances, a few monuments, the national anthem, and books that have been on the syllabus for decades. It is worth a fixed list. It is not worth an afternoon of obscure one-off facts.

UNESCO’s world-heritage list grows. Learn a few certain sites, and do not memorise a total that may already be out of date.

What SSC lists

  • Culture of India.
  • Classical dances, symbols, and a few books and monuments.
  • Facts that stay still, as distinct from this month’s awards.
  • Current awards and appointments belong on the current-affairs page’s method, not in a frozen list.

Rules and formulas

Dances

The eight classical dances in the Sangeet Natak Akademi list long used in exams are Bharatanatyam (Tamil Nadu), Kathak (north India, often linked with Uttar Pradesh), Kathakali (Kerala), Kuchipudi (Andhra Pradesh), Odissi (Odisha), Manipuri (Manipur), Sattriya (Assam) and Mohiniyattam (Kerala).

Symbols and songs

The national anthem Jana Gana Mana is by Rabindranath Tagore.
Vande Mataram is by Bankim Chandra Chatterjee.
The national animal in the standard answer is the tiger, the bird is the peacock, the flower is the lotus, and the fruit is the mango.
“National game” is a muddier label. Do not treat a guidebook line about hockey as a section of the Constitution.

Books

The Discovery of India is by Jawaharlal Nehru. India Wins Freedom is by Abul Kalam Azad. Wings of Fire is by A.P.J. Abdul Kalam. Godan is by Premchand.

Sites

The Taj Mahal, Ajanta, Ellora, Khajuraho, Hampi and the Sun Temple at Konark are stable monument names. The number of World Heritage sites changes, so revise the count from the UNESCO list if a question demands a number.

Common traps

Kathakali and Mohiniyattam are both from Kerala. They are not the same dance.

Sattriya is Assam. Manipuri is Manipur.

Jana Gana Mana is the anthem. Vande Mataram is the national song. Do not swap the authors.

A monument’s UNESCO status can be updated. The monument’s location does not change.

Worked examples

Read the solution only after you have tried the question. Options are in exam style.

Example 1
Bharatanatyam is a classical dance of

  1. Kerala
  2. Tamil Nadu
  3. Assam
  4. Manipur

Solution

  1. Bharatanatyam is Tamil Nadu.
  2. Kathakali is Kerala, Sattriya is Assam, and Manipuri is Manipur.

Answer: B. The state and the dance should be revised as a pair.

Example 2
Jana Gana Mana was written by

  1. Bankim Chandra Chatterjee
  2. Rabindranath Tagore
  3. Muhammad Iqbal
  4. Sarojini Naidu

Solution

  1. Tagore wrote Jana Gana Mana.
  2. Bankim Chandra Chatterjee wrote Vande Mataram.

Answer: B. Iqbal is associated with Saare Jahan Se Achha, a different song.

Example 3
The Discovery of India was written by

  1. Abul Kalam Azad
  2. Jawaharlal Nehru
  3. Mahatma Gandhi
  4. B.R. Ambedkar

Solution

  1. Nehru is the author.
  2. Azad wrote India Wins Freedom.

Answer: B. Keep the two freedom-movement books apart.

Example 4
Odissi is a classical dance of

  1. Odisha
  2. Andhra Pradesh
  3. Assam
  4. Uttar Pradesh

Solution

  1. The name points to Odisha.
  2. Kuchipudi is Andhra Pradesh and Kathak is the north-Indian form.

Answer: A. Sattriya, not Odissi, is the Assam dance.

Example 5
(CGL pattern) Which book is INCORRECTLY paired with its author?

  1. Hind Swaraj – Mahatma Gandhi
  2. Gitanjali – Rabindranath Tagore
  3. The Discovery of India – Subhas Chandra Bose
  4. Why I Am an Atheist – Bhagat Singh

Solution

  1. The Discovery of India is by Jawaharlal Nehru.
  2. The other three pairs are correct.

Answer: C. ‘Incorrectly paired’ questions reward a solid author list.

Example 6
(CGL pattern) Which Indian poet won the Nobel Prize in Literature in 1913?

  1. Aurobindo Ghose
  2. Rudyard Kipling
  3. Bankim Chandra Chattopadhyay
  4. Rabindranath Tagore

Solution

  1. Tagore won for Gitanjali in 1913.
  2. Kipling won in 1907, but he was a British writer.

Answer: D. Tagore was the first non-European Nobel laureate in Literature.
